I graduated from a double-degree PhD program at [*Huazhong Agricultural University*](https://www.hzau.edu.cn/) and [*University of Groningen*](https://www.rug.nl/). [*My PhD project*](https://hdl.handle.net/11370/a80e2530-098c-4543-8b92-1024b9f59425) explores the collective consequences of bounded rationality in opinion dynamics in networks. Currently I work as a postdoc in an EU project [*PREPSHIELD*](https://crimedim.uniupo.it/projects/prepshield), focusing on data analysis and simulating how human psychological and social features affect agents’ behaviors in a health crisis.

Beside science I like traveling and writing. I love experiencing different places and diversity. For me, the lifestyle and philosophy of the locals are the most fascinating part of traveling. Here is [***my footprint***](/footprint/liteng_footprint.html).
